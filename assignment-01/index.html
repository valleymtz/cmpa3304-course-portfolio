My Top 5 Books

  1. The Women by Kristin Hannah

    A historical fiction book about women veterans and the struggles they faced.

  2. The Last Letter by Rebecca Yarros

    An emotional romance filled with grief, love, and overcoming obstacles. This one comes with trigger warnings.

  3. A Love Letter to Whiskey by Kandi Steiner

    A love story about consistently bad timing. A true right person at the wrong time love story.

  4. Reminders of Him by Colleen Hoover

    A fictional romance about second chances.

  5. The Coworker by Freida McFadden

    A psychological thriller about a missing coworker and secrets that make you think you never really know someone.
