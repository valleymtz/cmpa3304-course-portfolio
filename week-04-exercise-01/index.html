In my "vandalism" of the Barnes & Noble homepage, I first changed in the elements panel one the headers to say "Books you can probably find cheaper on Amazon...no seriously". That was easy and fun so I continued making changes to the navigation menu names at the top of the page to things like, "boring books" and "not books". In the styles panel I had a little more trouble finding the element I wanted to change. I did end up finding and changing the font of the header to hot pink and a larger size. As well as the backgound color to yellow and red in some areas. It was more of a trial and error find to find the right element to change. When all was done,I ended up having a better understanding of how the HTML and CSS worked together to make the changes I made. I also have a better sense of how dev tools can be used while crafting my own webpages.
